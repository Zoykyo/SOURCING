# Evidence Review & Two Model Proposals

_Evidence-based critique of the SOURCING scoring model (5 categories × 25 sub-criteria), with two
candidate redesigns. Objective = balanced blend (quality of hire + placeability + retention),
evidence = academic-led, industry-supplemented._

Source: deep, multi-source research cycle (6 angles, 25 sources fetched, 103 claims extracted,
24 confirmed via 3-vote adversarial verification). Citations in §9.

---

## 1. The meta-finding: score with humility

The single most important result is a **calibration** finding, not a ranking one:

- **Legacy validity coefficients were overestimated.** The canonical Schmidt & Hunter rankings
  everyone quotes were corrected downward by **.10–.20** once range-restriction overcorrection was
  fixed (Sackett et al. 2022). General cognitive ability fell from ~.51 to **~.31**; **structured
  interviews are now the #1 predictor (~.42)**. _(Contested by Oh et al. 2023, who argue the
  correction went too far — so treat numbers as a range, not truth.)_
- **The best predictors are NOT observable at sourcing time.** Structured interviews (top for
  performance) and job attitudes / job embeddedness (top for retention) require interview or survey
  data you don't have when sourcing. **Everything our model can see from a profile is a weaker proxy.**

➡️ **Implication for the whole tool:** a sourcing score is a *coarse pre-screen for ranking who to
contact first* — not a validated selection instrument. The model should avoid false precision, lean
on the few decent observable proxies, and never be the sole basis for a hire decision.

---

## 2. The structural finding: quality ≠ retention (don't collapse them)

> Signals that predict **retention** do **not** automatically predict **quality of hire**.
> Applicant biodata predicted turnover well but performance weakly/inconsistently (Breaugh 2014).
> Performance itself predicts turnover only *indirectly* via job attitudes, embeddedness, and ease of
> movement (Han et al. 2024 MASEM, N=524,740).

Our current model blends all three outcomes into **one composite score**. The evidence says the three
outcomes are distinct and a high score on one does not imply the others. **This is the core argument
for Path 2** (separate the axes) over Path 1 (one blended score).

## 3. Key evidence (confirmed claims, with confidence)

| # | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Validity coefficients overestimated; corrected down .10–.20. Don't anchor weights to legacy numbers. | High |
| E2 | Structured interviews now #1 (~.42); GMA .31; job knowledge .40, biodata .38, work sample .33. Resume-observable signals are weaker than assumed. | High |
| E3 | Performance → turnover is mediated (desirability of movement strongest, then embeddedness, then ease of movement). Performance is not a standalone retention signal. | High |
| E4 | **Job embeddedness** has incremental validity for retention beyond satisfaction/commitment/alternatives. Observable proxies only: location/community ties, tenure, local studies+work region. | High |
| E5 | Retention-predicting signals ≠ quality-predicting signals. Don't collapse into one score. | Medium (single-study) |
| E6 | **Age/generation is NOT a valid job-hopping predictor** once career stage is controlled (median tenure 25–34 ≈ 2.7 yr, ~unchanged since 1983). Tenure must be career-stage normalized. | High |
| E7 | LinkedIn **Open-to-Work** is a real, observable placeability signal (effect size unproven). | Medium (vendor doc) |
| E8 | **Legal risk:** graduation-year & "willingness to learn"/"flexibility" = ADEA age-proxy risk; school prestige & location can create Title VII adverse impact unless validated job-related. | High |

**What the research could NOT confirm:** the practitioner claims about the "itch" / tenure-timing
windows and what drives passive-candidate *reply rates* — these remain **opinion, not validated**.
So the entire Placeability dimension rests on weaker evidence and should be weighted humbly.

## 4. Evidence map — the current 25 sub-criteria, graded

Grades per outcome: **S**trong / **M**oderate / **W**eak / **–** none. ⚠️ = fairness/legal flag.

### 1. Professional Profile & Career Trajectory
| Sub-criterion | Quality | Place | Retention | Verdict / action |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|
| Employers – Key OEM | M / W | – | – | Relevant-experience proxy = M; *prestige* = W ⚠️. Reframe as domain relevance, not prestige. |
| Relevant roles / title match | M | – | – | Job-knowledge proxy. **Keep, up-weight.** |
| Seniority vs. sought | W | – | – | Mostly a fit filter → lean toward a gate, low weight. |
| Career progression pattern | W | – | W | Weak evidence. Low weight. |
| Sub-domain match | M–S | – | – | Best domain-fit proxy. **Up-weight.** |

| Sub-criterion | Quality | Place | Retention | Verdict / action |

| Knowledge & standards (SAE/ISO/AS9100) | M–S | – | – | Job-knowledge, domain-specific. **Keep high.** |
| Education / continuing ed | W–M | – | – | Modest validity; ⚠️ strip school *prestige*. Keep small. |
| Language proficiency | – | – | – | A requirement, not a predictor. **Make a conditional gate.** |
| Associations / certifications | M (certs) / W (assoc.) | – | – | Keep certs (P.Eng = job-related); drop associations. |

### 3. Credibility, Reputation & Distinction

| Probability to stay | – | – | M | Right idea; **redefine via embeddedness proxies.** |

### 5. Mobility
| Sub-criterion | Quality | Place | Retention | Verdict / action |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|
| Interest in relocation | – | M | – | Feasibility. **Convert to gate.** |
| Willingness to travel | – | M | – | Feasibility. **Convert to gate.** |
| Location proximity | – | M | M | ⚠️ Embeddedness/retention proxy + feasibility. Keep small. |

**Two cross-cutting observations:**
- **Category 3 (Credibility) is the weakest category** for quality-of-hire — mostly prestige signals,
  except publications/patents. Strong candidate for shrinking/merging.
- **Category 5 (Mobility) is doing two unrelated jobs:** hard feasibility filters (relocation, travel,
  work mode) and retention/embeddedness proxies (location, region). These should be separated.

## 5. Path 1 — Refined (keep the 5-category spine)

Lowest disruption. Same familiar structure; evidence-driven re-weighting + targeted edits.

**Category weight changes (proposed):**
| Category | Now | Path 1 | Why |
|---|:--:|:--:|---|
| 1. Professional Profile | 0.22 | **0.24** | Job-knowledge proxies (roles, sub-domain) matter |
| 2. Skills & Qualifications | 0.30 | **0.34** | Best observable quality proxies — up |
| 3. Credibility & Distinction | 0.13 | **0.08** | Mostly weak prestige — down |
| 4. Availability & Retention | 0.20 | **0.22** | Placeability + retention — slight up |
| 5. Mobility | 0.15 | **0.12** | Mostly feasibility (some become gates) — down |

**Criteria edits (within the 25):**
- Reframe *Employers–OEM* as **relevant domain experience**, not prestige; up-weight sub-domain & relevant-roles.
- Convert *relocation, travel, work-mode* from scored items to **hard feasibility gates**.
- Redefine *Probability to stay* using **embeddedness proxies** (location/region/tenure ties).
- **Career-stage-normalize** *Tenure windows*; strip **school prestige** from *Education*.
- Merge weak distinction items (*awards + competition + authority*) into one "recognition" sub-criterion; keep *publications/patents* distinct.
- Add fairness flags to the ⚠️ items.

**Pros:** familiar to recruiters, fast to ship, minimal retraining. **Cons:** still produces one blended
score that conflates quality/placeability/retention — the exact thing E5 warns against.

## 6. Path 2 — Redesign (evidence-first)

Reorganizes the model around what the evidence actually says: separate **eligibility gates** from
**three distinct scored outcomes**, and report them **separately** instead of one blended number.

### A. Eligibility gates (pass/fail — not scored)
Must-have skills · location/work-mode/relocation/travel feasibility · language requirement ·
**work authorization** _(missing today — add)_ · exclusion list.

### B. Three scored axes (each 0–100, shown separately + an overall)
**1. Role Fit & Capability** — _quality proxy, highest weight (~0.45)_
  - Skills/competency match · sub-domain & domain fit · standards/technical knowledge ·
    relevant role & scope · capability evidence (publications/patents/portfolio, certifications).

**2. Placeability** — _likelihood to engage & move (~0.30, flagged lower-confidence)_
  - Open-to-work / availability · tenure-timing *(career-stage normalized)* · recent activity /
    engagement with firm-clients · warm contact / existing relationship.

**3. Retention / Stickiness** — _embeddedness proxy (~0.25)_
  - Location & community ties · local studies+work region · career-stage-normalized tenure pattern ·
    domain continuity.

### C. Fairness & confidence layer (governs all axes)
  - Block/limit legal-risk signals (no school prestige; normalize age proxies).
  - **Data-completeness / confidence weighting** — a score from 3 known fields ≠ a score from 15.
  - Each axis reported on its own so a recruiter sees *why* (great Placeability but weak Fit = different
    action than the reverse).

**Pros:** faithful to the evidence; separates outcomes; far better fairness posture; richer recruiter
signal. **Cons:** bigger conceptual change; more to build; recruiters must learn a 3-axis readout.

---

## 7. Path 1 vs Path 2

| Dimension | Path 1 (Refined) | Path 2 (Redesign) |
|---|---|---|
| Fidelity to evidence | Partial — re-weights but still one blended score | High — separates quality/placeability/retention |
| Fairness / legal posture | Improved (flags + normalization) | Strongest (gates + confidence + governance) |
| Disruption to recruiters | Low | Medium |
| Build effort | Low (weights + a few edits) | Medium (restructure engine + reporting) |
| Risk of false precision | Still present | Mitigated (humble, multi-axis, confidence-weighted) |
| Time to ship | Days | 1–2 short phases |

**My recommendation:** **Path 2 as the target, reached incrementally.** Ship Path 1's re-weighting and
gates now (immediate improvement, low risk), then evolve to the 3-axis readout — because E5 (quality ≠
retention) is the finding most likely to mislead recruiters if we keep one blended number. A hybrid gets
80% of the value fast without a disruptive big-bang.

---

## 8. Open questions the research could not close

1. Effect size of Open-to-Work / recent-activity / tenure-timing on **actual reply & acceptance rates** —
   unproven; needs dedicated industry data. (Placeability weighting is therefore provisional.)
2. Do these (cross-occupational) findings replicate in **aerospace/automotive engineering** specifically?
   No evidence is engineering-specific.
3. Which embeddedness proxies carry **real** predictive validity vs. merely plausible — and do they
   double-count Mobility?
4. Validated predictive power of **patents/publications/portfolio** for engineering quality specifically.
